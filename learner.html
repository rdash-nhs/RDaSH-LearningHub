Self Service Training Portal

The Self Service Learning Portal supports staff, managers and administrators in managing learning records through the Electronic Staff Record (ESR). Use the guidance on this page to enrol onto learning opportunities, register staff for courses, and record external learning activities to help maintain accurate learning records.

Employee Self-Enrolment Guide

1

Access My Learning

On your ESR dashboard, click the My Learning link in the sidebar to open your learner homepage.

2

Search for Training

Use the search bar on your learner page. Type in a keyword from the course name and click the magnifying glass icon.

3

Select and Enrol

Select your course to see available classes. Click the Enrol Cog for the specific class you want. Review the details, add any special instructions, and click Submit.

4

Check Enrollment

The course will now appear in your Instructor-Led Enrolments section on your learning home page.

How to Cancel

Click the Class Name on your learning page. Use the drop-down to select Unenrol, click Go, select your reason, and click Finish.

Manager & Admin Enrolment Guide

1

Access Team Learning

Click the Manager tab, select My Team Career Information, and then click Learning.

2

Identify Staff Member

Search for the staff member by name or select them from the list. Click the Action button (circled in yellow) to proceed to their learner page.

3

Search & Enrol

Type the course keyword into the Search Course bar. Select the correct class by clicking the Enrol Cog.

4

Confirmation

Add any special instructions and click Submit. The course will now show in the staff member's Instructor-Led Enrolments section.

How to Cancel

Click the Class Name, select Unenrol from the drop-down, and click Go. Choose a reason and click Finish.

Record External Learning

1

Navigate to External Classes

Click on My Learning in the sidebar, then click on manage my external classes at the bottom of the page.

2

Add a New Record

Click on the Add button to begin entering a new external class.

3

Enter Course Details

  • Always prefix the title with LHD – and free type the name of the course.
  • Select the date of the Learning Half Day on which you completed the course.
  • Free type the name of the supplier of the course.
  • Click Continue.
4

Review and Submit

  • The next page will confirm the details you have entered.
  • It will show who it will go to for approval to be added to ESR.
  • You will have the opportunity to add any comments for the approver.
  • Click on Submit to proceed (or Back to make changes).
5

Confirmation

You will receive a confirmation of submission. Click on Home to return to ESR.

Record Self Directed Learning

1

Navigate to Course Catalogue

In the navigation bar on the top of the Learning Hub website, click Course Catalogue.

2

Search Courses

Scroll down to the Course Prospectus and use the search bar to find Self Directed Study Declaration.

3

Book Course

Click on Self Directed Study Declaration which will expand the box. You can then click Book on the appropriate date.

4

Log In

You will be taken to the NHS Electronic Staff Record website. Use your ESR details to log in.

5

Select Date

Click on the Enrol cog on the correct date to enrol.

6

Submit

Enter details about your learning in the box titled Special Instructions and then press Submit.
